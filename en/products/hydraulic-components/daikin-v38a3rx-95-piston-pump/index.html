FAQ

Is V38A3RX-95 enough information for ordering a replacement pump?

It is a useful model reference, but buyers should also confirm displacement, pressure rating, rotation direction, shaft specification, port details, mounting dimensions and equipment application before ordering.

What is the complete reference for this DAIKIN piston pump?

Use model V38A3RX-95 together with MFG No. YED00-2511-00070 when requesting a quotation or checking a replacement.

Can a similar-looking piston pump be used as a replacement?

Not automatically. Similar appearance does not confirm hydraulic rating, shaft, ports, mounting, rotation direction or system compatibility.
